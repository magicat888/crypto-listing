# 31 — Head of Listing Monthly Report Template

> **Purpose.** Standard monthly report that the Head of Listing submits to the TARC and the Board, matching HKbitEX's existing template (`4010_20260903_HKbitEX_Monthly Report_Head of Listing.docx`) and the content requirements in HKbitEX TAP Appendix D [CIT-HKX-TAP-014].

---

## How to use this template

1. **Monthly cadence:** generated by the 10th Business Day of each month for the prior calendar month
2. **Owner:** Head of Listing (preparer), reviewed by Head of Risk Management and Head of Compliance before TARC meeting
3. **Distribution:** TARC (full meeting pack) + Board
4. **Storage:** Tamper-evident per `42-ai-agent-document-versioning.md`. Retention 7 years minimum
